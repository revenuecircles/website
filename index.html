Questions leaders ask first

Who is it for?

CROs, CCOs and VPs of Revenue leading commercial organizations at $50M+ PE‑backed or founder‑led B2B companies.

What does vetted mean?

Every applicant is assessed on seniority, company context and contribution to the Circle, in two rounds: the Regional Circle President first, then the two founding partners. All three vote. Referrals carry weight, and every applicant still goes through both rounds.

Does this help beyond my current role?

Yes. Senior roles are often filled through people who already know your work. Your Circle builds those relationships before you need them and prepares you for a bigger seat, in this company or the next.

Does Revenue Circles™ earn from Expert Access?

No. You hire experts directly, and Revenue Circles™ takes no fee.
