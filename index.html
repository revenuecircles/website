FAQ

Who is it for?

Revenue leaders in their first or second time owning the number at $50M+ B2B companies, whatever the title, and veteran revenue leaders.

I have held the seat three or more times. Is this for me?

Yes. You develop and challenge leaders new to the seat, and learn from them too.

What does vetted mean?

Every candidate is assessed on the number, generosity, discretion and curiosity, in two rounds: the Regional Circle President first, then the two founding partners. All three vote. Referrals carry weight, and every candidate still goes through both rounds.

Where do the dinners happen?

In Miami for the first Circles. The venue is shared at placement.

Can my company pay?

Yes. Payment is covered in the fit conversation.

Can you recommend outside help?

Yes. Members can see a short list of vetted firms and fractional leaders and contact them directly. Firms can apply by email.

What topics come up?

Whatever Members bring, with featured speakers from operating roles, private equity and leading business schools. For example:

  • Forecasts that hold up in the boardroom
  • Revenue durability before a sale, raise or acquisition
  • Pricing and packaging AI features
  • Net revenue retention through renewal and expansion
  • What veteran CROs would do differently in their first year
  • What PE operating partners look for in a CRO

Does this help beyond my current role?

Yes. Senior roles are often filled through people who already know your work. Your Circle is where they get to know it, and it prepares you for a bigger seat, in this company or the next.
